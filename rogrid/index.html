Game

Rogrid

Block puzzle with a roguelike twist

Fill the board, clear lines, and every few lines pick a perk that changes the run. Perks stack until the board comes alive.

Coming soon to the App StoreHow it plays

Free · optional purchases · iOS 17+

Every run is a build

The classic block puzzle loop, plus one decision that keeps every run different.

  1. 01

    Place

    Drag three pieces onto the 8×8 board. The tray refills once all three are down, so every drop is a plan.

  2. 02

    Clear

    Full rows and columns vanish. Two lines at once make a combo; consecutive clears build a streak multiplier up to ×3.

  3. 03

    Pick a perk

    Cleared lines fill the XP bar. When it fills, the run pauses and you choose one of three perks. They stack.

15 perks, four rarities

Rows that erase themselves, a bomb you aim, a curse that multiplies the score, fireworks that chain clears. The build you assemble is the run you get.
