LOCAL CREATOR OPERATIONS

ClipOps

A local desktop workflow for preparing short videos, managing authorized YouTube channels and submitting approved public links to Content Rewards.

What the application does

  • Verifies the operator's Google account email and the exact YouTube channel selected during authorization.
  • Uploads reviewed local videos privately, checks their processing and metadata, and publishes them only under the operator's explicit approval.
  • Reads channel/video details and native YouTube statistics and analytics to support the operator's own channel work.
  • Maintains local channel setup, publication and submission records so interrupted work can be reconciled without duplicate uploads.

Runs on the operator's computer

The application is a command-line tool for authorized operators. Google sign-in uses the browser and a local loopback callback. OAuth credentials are encrypted locally with a key protected by macOS Keychain. This information site does not receive Google tokens, account data or video files.

Google and Content Rewards are separate connections

The application's Google authorization supports YouTube uploads and analytics. Content Rewards uses its own account connection and terms. When instructed, the workflow submits the chosen public YouTube link and campaign information to Content Rewards; it does not send the application's Google OAuth tokens to that service.
